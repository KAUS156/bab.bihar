Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register my school?

Visit the Bihar Academic Battle website and open the School Registration page. Contact support if you need help.

How can I get help with my student registration?

Contact our support team by phone or WhatsApp and explain your registration issue.

How can I ask about certificates or results?

Contact support and provide the relevant student ID or registration details. Do not share your password or OTP.

How can a school get technical support?

School representatives can contact support for login, student records, examination and admit card assistance.
